# Multi-Year Cleanup — Service-Based Firm

## Business Profile

A professional or service-based firm earning revenue through retainers, projects, recurring engagements, hourly work, or fixed-fee services while using employees, contractors, software, and reimbursable expenses.

## Common Historical Issues

- Deposits recorded without connection to invoices or customers
- Retainers, prepaid services, and earned revenue combined
- Duplicate revenue from invoices and bank-feed deposits
- Open AR and customer credits not supported
- Contractor, payroll, and service-delivery costs inconsistently classified
- Reimbursable expenses mixed with normal operating costs
- Owner or partner activity recorded through operating expenses
- Loans, fixed assets, payroll liabilities, and credit cards not reconciled
- Customer, class, location, or project tracking used inconsistently
- Historical financials that do not support service-line or client profitability

## Cleanup Workflow

### Revenue and Customer Review

Organize historical income by customer, engagement, and revenue type where support is available.

### Billing, Retainers, and Receivables

Review invoices, payments, retainers, customer credits, write-offs, and open receivables while removing duplicate revenue.

### Service-Delivery Costs

Evaluate employee, contractor, software, travel, reimbursable, and other costs associated with delivering client work.

### Owner and Balance-Sheet Activity

Reconcile cash, credit cards, loans, payroll liabilities, fixed assets, owner contributions, distributions, and historical equity balances.

### Reporting Structure

Assess customer, class, location, department, or project tracking and preserve only structures supported by reliable historical information.

### Year-by-Year Close

Complete reconciliations, corrections, and annual rollforwards in chronological order and prepare supported reporting.

## Final Deliverables

- Corrected financial statements by year
- Revenue and receivables review
- Retainer and customer-credit schedule, when applicable
- Payroll, contractor, and service-delivery cost summary
- Owner-activity and balance-sheet schedules
- Client, project, or service-line reporting where support is available
- Cleanup adjustment and open-items reports
- Recommended ongoing accounting and close structure
