# Multi-Year Cleanup — Omnichannel E-Commerce

## Business Profile

An e-commerce or consumer-products company selling across a direct website, marketplaces, social commerce, wholesale accounts, or national retail while receiving funds through multiple processors and settlement systems.

## Common Historical Issues

- Net deposits recorded as revenue
- Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, TikTok Shop, Meta, Faire, or wholesale activity recorded inconsistently
- Duplicate sales created by connectors or manual entries
- Processor fees, refunds, reserves, and chargebacks omitted or misclassified
- Marketplace and processor clearing balances left unresolved
- Sales tax recorded as revenue or not reconciled
- Inventory and COGS that do not reflect actual product activity
- Wholesale receivables, deductions, or short pays missing from the ledger
- 1099-K totals that do not align with accounting records
- Channel activity crossing month-end or year-end periods

## Cleanup Workflow

### Historical Channel Map

Document which channels, processors, banks, connectors, inventory systems, and wholesale relationships were active during each cleanup year.

### Revenue and Settlement Reconstruction

Rebuild activity by channel so gross sales, discounts, refunds, taxes, fees, chargebacks, reserves, and net cash remain visible.

### Cash and Clearing Review

Reconcile processor and marketplace settlements to bank deposits and identify supported timing items or unresolved differences.

### Inventory and COGS Review

Evaluate available inventory records, purchasing activity, product costs, freight, fulfillment, returns, shrinkage, and historical adjustments.

### Wholesale and Retail Review

Reconstruct customer invoices, remittances, deductions, credits, short pays, unapplied cash, and remaining receivables where applicable.

### Year-by-Year Close

Complete balance-sheet reconciliations, cleanup adjustments, channel reporting, and year-end rollforwards before advancing to the next period.

## Final Deliverables

- Corrected financial statements by year
- Channel revenue and settlement reconciliation
- Processor and marketplace clearing schedules
- Sales-tax and liability review
- Inventory and COGS cleanup summary
- Wholesale AR and deduction schedule, when applicable
- Cleanup adjustment and open-items reports
- Recommended ongoing e-commerce close structure
