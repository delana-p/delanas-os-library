# Multi-Year Cleanup — Marketing Consultant

## Business Profile

An independent marketing consultant or boutique practice earning project, retainer, campaign, or advisory revenue while using contractors, software platforms, advertising accounts, and reimbursable client expenses.

## Common Historical Issues

- Client deposits recorded without identifying the related project or invoice
- Retainers, project fees, and reimbursable expenses combined
- Client advertising spend recorded as operating expense without reviewing reimbursement treatment
- Contractor payments inconsistently categorized or unsupported
- Business and personal activity mixed in the same accounts
- Duplicate income from invoices and bank-feed deposits
- Merchant fees and payment-platform activity omitted
- Software subscriptions spread across inconsistent categories
- Open invoices, customer credits, or retainers not supported
- Contractor and tax-reporting records incomplete

## Cleanup Workflow

### Revenue Review

Organize historical income by client and revenue type, including projects, retainers, advisory services, campaign work, and reimbursed costs.

### Client Billing and Cash

Review invoices, deposits, payment-platform activity, customer credits, and open receivables while removing duplicated revenue.

### Contractor and Delivery Costs

Identify contractor, freelancer, creative-production, media, and project-delivery costs and align them with the appropriate operating presentation.

### Reimbursable and Pass-Through Activity

Separate business operating expenses from client-funded advertising, production, travel, or other reimbursable activity.

### Balance-Sheet and Owner Activity

Reconcile cash, credit cards, loans, tax liabilities, owner contributions, owner draws, fixed assets, and other historical balances.

### Year-by-Year Close

Complete corrections, reconciliations, and rollforwards in chronological order and prepare reliable annual reporting.

## Final Deliverables

- Corrected financial statements by year
- Revenue summary by client or service type, when support is available
- AR, customer-credit, and retainer review
- Contractor and project-delivery cost summary
- Reimbursable-expense review
- Owner-activity and balance-sheet schedules
- Cleanup adjustment and open-items reports
- Recommended ongoing accounting and close structure
