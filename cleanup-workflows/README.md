# Multi-Year Cleanup Workflows

> Portfolio workflows for rebuilding historical accounting records into reliable, year-by-year financials.

Multi-year cleanup work is more than recategorizing transactions. Each accounting period must be reconstructed in sequence so opening balances, cash, liabilities, equity, revenue, expenses, and industry-specific balances carry forward correctly.

## Multi-Year Cleanup Framework

### 1. Scope and Historical Coverage

- Confirm the cleanup years and reporting requirements
- Identify all bank, credit-card, loan, payroll, payment, and operating systems used during each period
- Gather available statements, tax filings, prior financials, sales reports, payroll records, and supporting schedules
- Identify missing periods and unresolved access requirements

### 2. Accounting-File Stabilization

- Review the chart of accounts and tracking structure
- Identify duplicated, uncategorized, misclassified, deleted, or manually altered activity
- Evaluate opening balances and prior-period carryforwards
- Separate current cleanup activity from historical corrections

### 3. Balance-Sheet Reconciliation

- Reconcile cash and credit-card activity
- Rebuild loans, liabilities, payroll balances, taxes, receivables, payables, and fixed assets as applicable
- Review clearing, suspense, undeposited-funds, and uncategorized balances
- Establish supported year-end balances before moving into the next year

### 4. Industry-Specific Reconstruction

Each business follows a customized cleanup path based on how it earns revenue, receives cash, incurs costs, tracks customers or projects, and reports performance.

### 5. Year-by-Year Close

- Complete corrections in chronological order
- Confirm that each closing balance supports the following year's opening balance
- Separate supported timing items from unresolved differences
- Produce a documented open-items list for anything requiring additional evidence or a management decision

### 6. Reporting and Handoff

- Corrected profit and loss and balance sheet by year
- Reconciliation and supporting schedules
- Cleanup adjustment summary
- Open-items and follow-up list
- Recommendations for the ongoing accounting structure and monthly close

## Portfolio Note
