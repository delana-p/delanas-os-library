## Executive Reporting Views

### Revenue

- Revenue by subscription, support, implementation, or other defined stream
- Revenue by enterprise and self-serve motion
- Actual results compared with plan and prior period

### Cash and Runway

- Current cash
- Near-term customer collections
- Planned operating disbursements
- Rolling forecast
- Burn and runway sensitivity

### Accounts Receivable

- Open and overdue balances
- Largest customer receivables
- Expected collection timing
- Billing or collection follow-up

### SaaS Performance

- MRR and ARR
- New, expansion, contraction, and churn
- Customer retention indicators
- Gross margin
- Customer and revenue concentration

### Operating Expenses

- Actual versus plan
- Department or account-level drivers
- Recurring versus nonrecurring spend
- Material changes requiring management attention

## Portfolio Note

The figures above are synthetic and are not associated with any client or company.
