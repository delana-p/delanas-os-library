# Workflow 04 — Lean SaaS Finance + Executive Reporting

## Business Profile

A growing SaaS company that needs current finance visibility without building an unnecessarily heavy technology stack.

## Financial Operations Scope

- QuickBooks Online accounting actuals
- Accounts receivable and cash tracking
- Rolling cash-flow forecasting
- Burn and runway monitoring
- Subscription and revenue reporting
- Budget-to-actual analysis
- Customized SaaS KPI reporting
- Executive dashboards and finance question support

## Executive Experience

Leadership receives a current view of:

- Cash and forecast runway
- Open and overdue receivables
- MRR and ARR
- New, expansion, contraction, and churn activity
- Revenue and gross margin
- Operating expenses versus plan
- Material financial changes and follow-up items

The reporting environment can also support a read-only finance interface in a collaboration platform, allowing leadership to ask questions using current accounting actuals and approved reporting models.

## Portfolio Outcome

A practical finance function that gives leadership faster access to reliable accounting, cash, forecast, and SaaS performance information while preserving financial review and oversight.
