# Workflow 01 — Self-Serve Subscription SaaS

## Business Profile

A high-growth SaaS company with monthly and annual self-serve subscriptions, online billing, payment processing, refunds, disputes, plan changes, and a large volume of recurring transactions.

## Financial Operations Scope

- Subscription billing and collections
- Processor settlement and cash reconciliation
- Monthly and annual prepaid subscriptions
- Deferred and recognized revenue reporting
- Refund, dispute, cancellation, and plan-change visibility
- MRR and ARR movement reporting
- Month-end accounting and management reporting

## Management Visibility

The reporting package distinguishes recurring revenue performance from cash collection timing and provides visibility into:

- Beginning and ending MRR
- New and expansion MRR
- Contraction and churn
- Annual versus monthly billing mix
- Deferred revenue
- Refund and dispute trends
- Settlement and cash activity

## Portfolio Outcome

A repeatable monthly view of subscription revenue, cash, deferred revenue, and customer movement that supports accounting close and management decision-making.
