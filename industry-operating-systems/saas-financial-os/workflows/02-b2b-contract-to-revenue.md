# Workflow 02 — B2B Contract-to-Revenue

## Business Profile

A sales-assisted B2B SaaS company with annual or multi-year customer agreements, scheduled billing, implementation or support components, accounts receivable, renewals, and contract changes.

## Financial Operations Scope

- Contract and order-form financial review
- Billing schedules and invoice oversight
- Accounts receivable and cash application
- Deferred and recognized revenue schedules
- Contract renewal and modification tracking
- Bookings, billings, cash, and revenue reporting
- Month-end close and management reporting

## Management Visibility

The reporting package keeps related but different commercial and accounting measures visible:

- Signed contract value
- Current and future billings
- Open accounts receivable
- Cash collected
- Revenue recognized
- Deferred revenue
- Renewals and contract changes

## Portfolio Outcome

A clear contract-to-financial-reporting view that supports reliable billing, AR oversight, revenue reporting, and executive understanding of commercial performance.
