# Workflow 03 — Hybrid B2B + Self-Serve SaaS

## Business Profile

A SaaS company serving enterprise customers through negotiated contracts while also offering online monthly or annual subscriptions.

## Financial Operations Scope

- Enterprise contract billing and AR
- Self-serve subscription billing and settlements
- Revenue and deferred-revenue reporting by motion
- Consolidated accounting and management reporting
- Customer migration between self-serve and enterprise plans
- Segmented MRR, ARR, churn, and expansion reporting
- Month-end close across both revenue models

## Management Visibility

The reporting package presents enterprise and self-serve performance separately before consolidation, allowing leadership to evaluate:

- Revenue and recurring revenue by motion
- AR versus processor settlement activity
- Customer growth, expansion, contraction, and churn
- Enterprise and self-serve billing trends
- Customer movement between business models
- Consolidated revenue and deferred revenue

## Portfolio Outcome

One management view of the company without losing the distinct economics and cash patterns of enterprise and self-serve customers.
