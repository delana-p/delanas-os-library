# SaaS Financial OS

> A portfolio build for subscription, sales-assisted, hybrid, and lean SaaS finance environments.

Designed by **Delana Prudhomme**, this project demonstrates the financial operations needed to connect recurring billing, customer contracts, cash, accounts receivable, revenue, SaaS metrics, forecasting, and executive reporting.

## Business Profiles Covered

| Profile | Representative environment | Financial focus |
|---|---|---|
| Self-serve subscription SaaS | High-volume monthly and annual subscriptions | Billing, settlements, deferred revenue, recurring revenue, and subscription metrics |
| B2B contract SaaS | Annual and multi-year sales-assisted agreements | Contracts, billing, AR, cash, revenue schedules, and management reporting |
| Hybrid B2B + self-serve SaaS | Contracted enterprise customers and online subscriptions | Separate revenue motions, consolidated reporting, and segmented SaaS metrics |
| Lean SaaS finance function | QBO, subscription systems, controlled reporting models, and collaboration tools | Cash, runway, AR, KPIs, budget-to-actuals, and executive finance access |

## What This Portfolio Demonstrates

- Subscription billing and cash reconciliation
- Deferred and recognized revenue oversight
- B2B contract, invoice, AR, and cash visibility
- Bookings, billings, cash, and revenue reporting
- MRR and ARR movement reporting
- New, expansion, contraction, and churn analysis
- Hybrid revenue reporting by customer motion
- Cash-flow forecasting, burn, and runway monitoring
- Budget-to-actual and operating-expense analysis
- QuickBooks Online alignment and month-end reporting
- Executive dashboards and finance question support
